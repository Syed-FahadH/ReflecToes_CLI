Can the driver stop?

Three drivers, all paying attention. Each one recognizes the person at the distance their clothing allows, takes 1.5 seconds to react, then does the best they can: move over, brake, or run out of road.

The approach

At 55 mph a driver needs about 265 feet to stop. A vest is recognized at 142.

Accuracy check
  • Pedestrian: black 18 ft, vest 142 ft, biomotion 486 ft (Wood 2005, low beams, closed road, as reported in Wood 2023).
  • Reaction time 1.5 s (a common estimate for an unexpected hazard); braking at 0.7 g (dry pavement, good tires). Stopping distance: 136 ft at 35 mph, 265 ft at 55 mph.
  • These test drivers knew they were looking for pedestrians, so real roads are worse. Worth saying on the page.
  • The live Biomotion page's ~55 / ~120 / ~410 ft mixes two studies. Recommend switching to one of the sets above.
