How a top 1% marketer would push these further

  1. One number per demo, framed as an outcome. Not "biomotion improves conspicuity" but "At 55 mph a driver needs 265 ft to stop. A vest gets you seen at 142." Feet, seconds and mph beat adjectives. (Done in 01–05.)
  2. Sell to people who already own a vest. That's the biggest pool of buyers. The Clemson result is the pitch: same vest plus reflective ankles = 1.9× the distance. Run it as a page headline, an ad and a bundle: "Already have a vest? Add socks."
  3. Make the reader do something. The quiz, the flash test and the gear builder turn a reader into a participant. People believe what they find out themselves. Put a demo in the first screen of each page, not halfway down.
  4. Every demo ends at a cart. After the gear builder or quiz, show "Your setup: socks + gloves, $X, 15% off as a kit" with one button. The demo is the product picker.
  5. Turn the demos into 6–10 second vertical videos. The 55 mph crash outcome and the "hide the body" moment are thumb-stoppers for Reels, TikTok and Meta ads. Same code, recorded to MP4 in Claude Code.
  6. #FlashTest user content. Ask buyers to post a phone-flash photo of their gear. Free proof, and it makes every competitor's "reflective" claim testable.
  7. Capture emails on the quiz. "Get your score + the 1-page night safety checklist." Feeds the Klaviyo flows.
  8. One set of numbers sitewide. Mixed studies on different pages (~410 ft here, 486 ft there) quietly cost trust. Pick one source per claim and cite it under the demo. Table below.
  9. Say the scary part honestly. "These drivers knew they were looking for pedestrians. Real drivers don't." It's true, it's from the studies, and it makes every number land harder.

How these ship (when you approve)

  • One shared theme asset (rt-figures.js, no libraries) plus one CSS file. Each demo becomes a theme section you can drop on any page from the theme editor. Scripts in a theme section run reliably, unlike scripts pasted into a page's rich-text body (which may be why the live demos are pure CSS).
  • Built and checked on an unpublished theme first.
  • Every demo starts on its own, like the live CSS animations. They pause only when scrolled off screen, to save battery.

Local preview: run python _serve.py 8766 in this folder, then open http://localhost:8766. Opening the files directly from disk also works.
