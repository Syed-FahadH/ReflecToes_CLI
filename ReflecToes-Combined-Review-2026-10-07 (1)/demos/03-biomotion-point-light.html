v2 changes from your notes
  • Rebuilt on the new gait engine (real joint-angle curves, smoothed). Walk, run and cycle all move naturally.
  • No knee or elbow lights. Reflective is only where people actually wear it: ankles (socks or bands), wrists (gloves), and the strap-style safety vest the studies actually tested (not our full reflective vest).
  • Side, behind (walking away) and front views. From the side the vest straps line up and shrink to a sliver, while the ankles keep moving. That matches the Clemson finding that side-on pedestrians were harder to spot.
  • Freeze works now, and it shows the matching stat: standing still was spotted 2.5× closer.
  • New "Vest + reflective ankles" mode, straight from the Clemson study. It's the pitch for adding socks to the vest people already own.
Note on numbers: these readouts come from one on-road study (Balk 2007) so they compare cleanly. The live page's ~55 / ~120 / ~410 ft come from different closed-track studies. Pick one set per page so the numbers don't contradict each other.
