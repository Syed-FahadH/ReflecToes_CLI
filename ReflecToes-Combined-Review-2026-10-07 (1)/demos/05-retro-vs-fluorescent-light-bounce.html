Where does the light go?

One headlight, three fabric samples, one at a time. Plain and fluorescent fabric scatter the light. Retroreflective sends it straight back to the driver's eyes.

Clemson University study

People bet on fluorescent yellow. In headlights, reflective looked 2.8× brighter.

2.8×brighter: retroreflective vs. fluorescent yellow, judged in a headlight-style beam12.5 vs 4.5 (King, Szubski & Tyrrell 2023)
2.5×dimmer than people predicted: fluorescent yellow at nightPredicted 11.0, judged 4.5
