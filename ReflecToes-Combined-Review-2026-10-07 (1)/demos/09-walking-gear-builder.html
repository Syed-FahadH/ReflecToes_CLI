v2 changes from your notes
  • "Full reflective vest": reflective on the front, back and sides, so it stays bright from every view.
  • LED clip lights are a 2-pack on velcro straps: pick ankles or wrists. They blink red and stay visible from every angle.
  • No center road lines; the walker is on an unlit road edge.
  • Three views: crossing (side), walking toward the car (front), walking away (back).
  • The status box now cites the research when it applies (for example, the Clemson "vest + ankles = 1.9×" result).
