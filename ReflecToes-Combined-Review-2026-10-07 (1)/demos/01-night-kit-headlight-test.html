The Headlight Test

A vest says "something's there." Moving ankles say "person."

v2 changes from your notes
  • New walking engine built from real gait curves (hip, knee and ankle angles over a stride), smoothed so there are no jerks. Feet stay planted while the body moves.
  • Strap vest is now a real 3D vest: X straps front and back, straps over the shoulders and a waist belt. From the side you see what you'd actually see: the shoulder straps and belt, not a front-facing X.
  • Headlights light up the pavement: beam cones plus light pooling on the road.
  • Starts by itself. Headlights come on automatically about a second after it scrolls into view.
  • Headline stats strip on top, all from the research folder.
