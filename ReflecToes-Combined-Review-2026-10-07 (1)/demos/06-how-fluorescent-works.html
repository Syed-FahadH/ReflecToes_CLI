Why fluorescent "glows" in daylight

Two yellow fabrics in the sun. Both bounce back the visible light. Only the fluorescent one also takes the invisible UV and sends it back as more yellow. That extra light is the glow.

How fluorescent works

Fluorescent turns light you can't see into color you can.

Sunlight carries ultraviolet (UV), which is invisible to the eye. Car headlights (halogen, HID and LED) produce almost no UV in the wavelengths that activate fluorescent dyes. Simplified illustration.
