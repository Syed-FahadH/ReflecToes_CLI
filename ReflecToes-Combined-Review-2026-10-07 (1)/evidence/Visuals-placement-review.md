# Visuals placement and marketing review

The strongest opportunity is to turn recognition science into a simple action: understand moving-joint placement, choose appropriate gear, and test its retroreflective response. The dark scenes and fluorescent contrast work well. Showing three large statistics before nearly every interactive module makes mobile visitors scroll past the actual demonstration. Use one headline, a compact source caption, the demonstration, and a relevant shopping action.

This review feeds the combined HTML, revision RTSEO-2026-10-07-R3. The ten originals remain unchanged and are review concepts, not approved production sections. The Claude artifact could not be verified through the web tool; its permissions, all ten demos, scripts, asset loading and commenting behavior are unverified. The portable review ZIP is the reproducible handoff.

## Verified issues in the new prototypes

1. **Data changes when Freeze is clicked.** Demo 03 divides every displayed distance by 2.5, including Wood dark-clothing data, while allowing running/cycling and all angles. Balk's 2.5 result is an aggregate walking-in-place versus standing result, not a universal per-outfit/angle multiplier. Make Freeze a playback control only. Keep measured clothing presets with population/conditions explicit; offer running/cycling/views as qualitative illustrations without transplanting pedestrian measurements.
2. **LEDs inherit reflective-straps evidence.** Demo 09 includes ankle LEDs in the `ankles` condition, and LED-plus-vest displays the 1.9x strap finding. Browser interaction reproduced it. Keep LED and passive retroreflective mechanisms distinct. Study comparison is not a measurement of ReflecToes' full vest plus arbitrary socks/lights.
3. **Quiz outfit does not match its answer.** Demo 07 shows socks/gloves but assigns the full-biomotion 486 ft result. Match the illustrated tested configuration. Demo 01 similarly places full-biomotion/recognition percentages next to Night Kit imagery; the source caption must make the measured setup clear.
4. **Generic fluorescence is shown as exactly doubling.** Demos 05/06 assert 'twice the light' and '1 in, 2 out.' Distinguish reflected visible light from extra visible emission sourced from absorbed UV. Use a qualitative indication or measured sample-specific data; do not imply photon/energy creation or universal identical headlight brightness for all fabrics. This concerns new prototype assertions; approved live supplier claims stay unchanged.
5. **Perceived brightness becomes apparent photometry.** Demo 05's 12.5/4.5 values are magnitude-estimation judgments, not cd/lx/m², detection distances or measured flux. Keep strong judged-brightness wording and clear study conditions. Line thickness is a schematic visual cue unless calibrated. It is not evidence that this particular product is 2.8x brighter.
6. **A conditional model becomes a guaranteed crash.** Demo 04's stopping calculations are approximately correct for 1.5 s reaction and 0.7 g braking: 135.5 ft at 35 mph and 265.5 ft at 55 mph. Recognition means are not deterministic visibility thresholds. The invented lane-change rule (`v*2.2`) and maneuver duration (1.8 s) have no supplied evidence. Prefer a recognition-distance chart with an explicitly assumed stopping band or extra-seconds display. If retaining a simulator, show assumptions at the point of use and require model review; do not advertise simulated crash outcomes as study results.
7. **No reduced-motion support or general pause control.** The shared engine explicitly ignores reduced-motion preferences. Demo 04 disconnects its intersection observer on first view, then loops offscreen. Restore static/replayable alternatives, pause/resume, offscreen and hidden-tab suspension. See https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html .
8. **Prototype architecture needs section-safe lifecycle.** Global `window.RT` replacement, generic IDs and global CSS are not reusable Shopify sections as-is. Use section instance roots, scoped styles, initialize once, destroy observers/timers/animation frames on section unload. Demo 04's Replay/speed handlers can retain queued animation frames; the quiz replaces canvases without engine cleanup. Do not build a broad framework; one small shared module with per-demo initialization is sufficient.
9. **Screen-reader and mobile presentation.** Demo 03 updates an aria-live number every frame; announce settled user-triggered changes. Demo 09 mixes links/buttons inside checkbox labels; use explicit associations. Chart/table headers need keyboard sorting and state. Stats stack ahead of the visual on narrow screens; move extra proof below the demonstration. Tiny road text in Demo 04 is difficult to read on mobile even without overflow. All data/meaning needs a readable text/static equivalent outside canvas.
10. **Flash test is qualitative.** Phone flash/camera and eye-level flashlight share near-source observation geometry, but do not reproduce road headlight geometry, distance, beam aim or driver recognition. Exposure and HDR affect apparent brightness. Keep the useful experiment; describe it as a check of retroreflective response. Do it away from traffic and avoid pointing light into eyes. Validate actual product appearance/mounting rather than depicting uniform 360-degree LED visibility.

## Source work completed during this review

- Drive `Improving the conspicuity and safety of pedestrians and cyclists on night-time roads...pdf`, PDF page 5, printed page 230: Wood's 2023 review reports 148.2 m biomotion, 43.4 m vest, 5.6 m black; Figure 2 identifies low-beam results. It also reports 100/60/40% recognition under low beams. This provides verified review-level support; retain the original 2005 bibliographic reference and distinguish this from an independently read original table.
- Drive `Nighttime Pedestrian Conspicuity_ The Effects of Pedestrian Movem.pdf`, PDF pages 42–44: Balk gives 79.2 m vest+ankles versus 40.8 m vest averaged across movement/orientation; walking 99.5 m versus standing 40.2 m aggregated across conditions. Configuration-specific motion comparisons differ: vest+ankles approximately 2x, vest approximately 4x, full biomotion approximately 2.6x. Applying 2.5 universally is demonstrably wrong.
- Drive King/Szubski/Tyrrell paper, PDF page 10: diffuse mean judged score 2.0, fluorescent 4.5, retroreflective 12.5; predictions 4.0, 11.0 and 6.0 respectively. These are perceptual scores, not radiometric measurements. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/00187208231210644 .
- New 51% non-dark cyclist fatality tile still needs its exact 2018–2022 aggregate and categories confirmed. A death share does not measure per-trip risk or the effectiveness of a daylight kit.
- The existing 55/120/410 ft chart's exact provenance remains unresolved. Leave it unchanged while tracing its original source. Do not replace it merely because the new demos use other supported numbers.

## Other ways to showcase the data for marketing

- **Already own a vest?** A compact research card compares the measured vest and vest+ankles conditions, then says 'Add reflective material at moving joints' with socks/bands options. Attribute the 1.9x result to the tested configuration; do not claim this exact shopping bundle produced it.
- **Expected versus observed:** show paired bars for King brightness judgments, with a reveal interaction and explicit 'perceived brightness scores' label. This sells the retroreflective mechanism better than treating simulated beam widths as product tests.
- **Extra time, transparently:** use a distance line and seconds gained at a chosen speed. Keep 'distance difference / speed' separate from reaction and braking assumptions. No lane-change/crash prediction is needed to make the benefit vivid.
- **Measured movement matrix:** a static walking-versus-standing chart for each Balk tested configuration conveys why movement matters and avoids multiplying every scene by one average ratio. Source/caption stays beside it.
- **Day and night pair:** same real gear photographed in daylight and with controlled flash, paired with the 05/06 explainer. State conditions; avoid presenting auto-exposed phone photos as quantitative superiority tests.
- **Short social sequences:** 6–10 second captioned clips showing 'hide the body' or a customer's flash test; finish with one specific product/guide action. Captions, poster frame and aspect-ratio-specific layout are necessary. Defer the simulated crash clip.
- **Source-linked carousel and email:** one result per slide, tested condition on the slide, full source in caption; use an existing-vest add-on or day/night explanation email. Prices, discounts, product availability and bundle offers require live validated data and owner approval. The suggested 15% kit discount is not approved.
- **Workshop and UGC:** printable flash-test card and QR link to workshop-kit; optional customer photo submissions with permission for reuse. Let quiz users see results before any optional email signup. No unsolicited messaging or uploads are part of this plan.

Measure demo start, meaningful interaction, completion and relevant shopping click separately from add-to-cart and purchases. Judge qualified shopping behavior and organic conversions, not animation engagement alone. Baseline and compare a small pilot; do not roll out all ten at once.
