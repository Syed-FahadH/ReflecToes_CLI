# ReflecToes Shopify claims and merchandising guardrails

## Never weaken claims (Ben correction 2026-10-07, highest priority)
- Do not remove, soften, or propose deleting a claim because no source is visible. Ben added many specifics personally.
- Unsourced claim: search Ben's Google Drive visibility research and the literature for the source. If not found, leave the claim exactly as is. Do not report it as a problem.
- Only fix actual inaccuracies or obvious mistakes, and keep the fixed claim as strong as the evidence allows.
- Different pages may use different studies (pedestrian vs cyclist, different comparators). Identify the study before calling numbers a conflict.
- Supplier specs (330+ and 720+ cd/lx/m2) are valid and private. Never ask Ben to share supplier documents.
- "Meets highway brightness standards" product copy is approved. Keep it.
- "10x brighter than color reflective" is Ben's own unpublished test knowledge (metalized mirror backing vs colored backing). Approved.
- Not every claim needs a citation. Think as a marketer: boilerplate can be improved for conversion, never stripped.

## Marketing claims
- Keep the strongest truthful claim. Do not weaken or delete a strong claim until the original research has been checked.
- Fix the metric, comparator, population, attribution, or test condition when needed.
- Quantified research claims should use small numbered superscript links to the matching anchor on `/pages/science-sources`.
- Do not imply that a university study tested a ReflecToes SKU unless it actually did.
- Manufacturer and supplier specifications are valid product facts even when the supporting supplier documents stay private.
- Old P0/P1/P2 labels are historical audit flags, not implementation instructions.

## Research anchors
- Balk et al. 2008 supports **up to 7x farther** in a specific pedestrian walking condition: about 117 m for full biomotion versus 17 m for an equal-area torso reflective configuration.
- Wood et al. 2012 cyclist data: about 386 ft moving-joint configuration, 126 ft reflective vest, and 65 ft black clothing. This is about 5.9x versus black and 3.1x versus vest.
- Do not say **6x faster** from Wood 2012. It measured recognition distance. Use **nearly 6x farther**.
- Wood et al. 2017 separately supports substantially shorter recognition time under the tested biomotion condition.
- Edewaard et al. 2020 supports **3.3x farther** daylight recognition for fluorescent jersey plus fluorescent leg covers versus the same jersey alone.
- Lahrmann et al. 2018 reported **55% fewer multiparty cyclist-motor-vehicle crashes** in the high-visibility jacket group. Attribute this to the randomized trial and do not imply ReflecToes itself was tested.
- Use Gunnar Johansson 1973 for the foundation of biological-motion research, not William Sturgeon.

## Product facts
- No ReflecToes product uses reflective yarn. Reflective socks use athletic yarn plus separately applied high-intensity retroreflective heat-transfer material.
- Reflective socks: 330+ cd/lx/m2 material specification, seven rows of reflective diamonds, 50+ wash durability, machine washable.
- RT-D1 and RT-D2: 330+ cd/lx/m2. No 10-year lifespan claim.
- RT-D2B and RT-D4: 720+ cd/lx/m2 and manufacturer-rated 10-year service life.
- Do not publish observation or entrance angles for 720 unless the exact supplier test geometry is verified.
- RT-JA jackets only: 15,000 mm waterproof and 9,000 g/m2/24h breathability. Do not copy those specs to discontinued RT-CJ.
- UPF/UV protection is a legitimate fabric feature where applicable. It is separate from retroreflectivity and fluorescent UV-reactive brightness.
- Supplier technical documents stay private.

## Care and warranty
- Reflective and fluorescent socks: machine washable in cold water.
- Most reflective outerwear, including jackets, vests, and gloves: hand wash cold or cool and hang dry.
- Summer cycling gloves: hand wash, hang dry, and close hook-and-loop straps before washing to reduce snagging.
- Deluxe Vest and Lightweight/Packable Vest are different products.
- Published warranty is 30 days.
- Defects reported within about one year are generally replaced as a customer-service accommodation. Do not call that a one-year warranty.

## Variant image rules
- Audit every product family with color, style, or construction variants.
- Do not change a variant image assignment unless confidence is at least 98% that the proposed image is the exact variant.
- If confidence is lower, show the current assignment and candidate exact Shopify images to Ben or his assistant for selection.
- Do not use a similar product as a substitute for a missing variant image.
- Known 2026-10-01 issues: reflective sock 3 Color variants and Orange variants have no variant media assigned; fluorescent Multi Color variants have no media assigned; XXL Non-Waterproof winter cycling gloves are assigned a waterproof image.

## Generated product imagery
- Never present a generated lookalike as a ReflecToes product.
- Start from an exact ReflecToes product image.
- Treat the source product as immutable. Preserve visible silhouette, seams, reflective placement, graphics, closures, straps, pockets, trim, logos, and color blocking.
- Do not invent a hidden side. If the source only shows the front, keep the camera angle to a view supported by that source.
- If a back view is needed, use an exact back-view source image.
- Do not blend features across product families. Deluxe Vest and Packable Vest are separate. Reflective and fluorescent socks are separate. Full-Finger and Half-Finger gloves are separate.
- If the generator cannot preserve the exact product after a few iterations, use a product-free educational graphic or an existing real lifestyle image instead.
- Every generated product image must be compared side by side with its source before publication.

## Writing and FAQ rules
- Do not use em dashes in ReflecToes site copy or project deliverables.
- Avoid generic FAQ filler. Each FAQ should answer a purchase objection, help choose a product, prevent a return, explain a meaningful product difference, or answer a real store-policy question.
- If a product or policy fact is not known at 98% confidence, ask Ben before publishing it.
- Use natural human wording. Avoid canned marketing filler and AI-sounding phrasing.
