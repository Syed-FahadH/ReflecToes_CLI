# ReflecToes Shopify claims guardrails

## Marketing standard
- Do not weaken or delete a strong claim before checking the original research.
- Keep the strongest truthful result. Fix the metric, comparator, population, attribution, or test condition instead.
- Research claims get a small numbered superscript linking to the exact anchor on `/pages/science-sources`.
- The marketing page stays clean. Full study details and original DOI/PubMed links live on the Science & Sources page.
- Never imply a study tested a ReflecToes SKU unless it actually did.
- Manufacturer and supplier specifications are valid product facts even when the supporting supplier documents stay private.

## Science claims
- Balk et al. 2008 supports **up to 7× farther** in a specific pedestrian walking condition: about 117 m for full biomotion versus 17 m for an equal-area torso reflective configuration. Do not present that as a universal 7× advantage over every commercial safety vest.
- Wood et al. 2012 cyclist data: 386 ft moving-joint configuration, 126 ft reflective vest, 65 ft black clothing. This is about 5.9× versus black and 3.1× versus vest.
- Do not say **6× faster** from Wood 2012. It measured recognition distance. Use **nearly 6× farther**.
- Wood et al. 2017 separately supports recognition in about half the time under the tested biomotion condition.
- Edewaard et al. 2020 supports **3.3× farther** daylight recognition for fluorescent jersey plus fluorescent leg covers versus the same jersey alone.
- Lahrmann et al. 2018 reported **55% fewer multiparty cyclist-motor-vehicle crashes** in the high-visibility jacket group. Attribute the result to the randomized trial and do not imply ReflecToes itself was tested.
- William Sturgeon is not the source of biological-motion research. Use Gunnar Johansson 1973.

## Product facts
- No ReflecToes product uses reflective yarn. Reflective socks use athletic yarn plus separately applied high-intensity retroreflective heat-transfer material.
- Reflective socks: 330+ cd/lx/m² material specification, seven rows of reflective diamonds, 50+ wash durability, machine washable.
- RT-D1 and RT-D2: 330+ cd/lx/m². No 10-year lifespan claim.
- RT-D2B and RT-D4: 720+ cd/lx/m² and manufacturer-rated 10-year service life.
- Do not publish observation/entrance angles for 720 unless the exact supplier test geometry is verified.
- RT-JA jackets only: 15,000 mm waterproof and 9,000 g/m²/24h breathability. Do not copy those specs to discontinued RT-CJ.
- UPF/UV protection is a legitimate fabric feature where applicable. It is separate from retroreflectivity and fluorescent UV-reactive brightness.
- Supplier technical documents stay private.

## Care and warranty
- Reflective and fluorescent socks: machine washable.
- Most reflective outerwear, including jackets, vests, and gloves: hand wash cold or cool and hang dry.
- Summer cycling gloves: hand wash, hang dry, and close hook-and-loop straps before washing so they do not snag other fabric.
- Deluxe Vest and Lightweight/Packable Vest are different products.
- Published warranty is 30 days.
- Defects reported within about one year are generally replaced as a customer-service accommodation. Do not call that a one-year warranty.

## Process lesson
- Old P0/P1/P2 cleanup labels are historical only. They are not implementation instructions.
- Before changing a quantitative claim, verify the original paper and use the correct study for that population.
- Keep a single authoritative implementation plan. Archive superseded plans so an agent does not follow stale recommendations.
