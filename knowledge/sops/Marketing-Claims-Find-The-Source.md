# Marketing claims: find the source, don't delete

Applies to every brand and every AI reviewing site copy. Shared with the team through the ai-business repo.

## Rule
- Review copy as a marketer whose job is to sell with true claims, not as a lawyer whose job is to remove risk.
- "No source on the page" is never a reason to delete or weaken a claim. Go find the source.
- Where to look, in order: the brand note and claims learnings, the live Science & Sources page (ReflecToes `/pages/science-sources`), supplier and manufacturer specs, then the original research (Google Scholar, PubMed, DOI).
- Found it: keep the claim, fix the exact number, comparator, or population if needed, and add the citation (ReflecToes: numbered superscript linking to the Science & Sources anchor; add a new source entry if needed).
- The research says something different: rewrite to the strongest version the research actually supports. Keep it punchy.
- Still can't find a source after a real search: leave the claim live, flag it for Ben with what you searched, and suggest the closest supported wording. Only Ben decides to remove a claim.

## Not acceptable
- "I don't see a source for this, let's delete it."
- Hedging good copy into mush ("may help", "could potentially") when the research supports a firm statement.
- Replacing a specific claim with generic filler.

## Still never allowed
Inventing numbers, studies, testimonials, or certifications, or implying a study tested our product when it did not. See [[ReflecToes-Shopify-Claims-Guardrails]].
