# Oh Susannah copy and image rules

Shared with the team through the ai-business repo. Brand facts and SEO status: [[Oh-Susannah]]. Primary domain `https://www.ohsusannah.com`.

## Copy rules
- Do not join synonymous item names with "and" or "&" in a way that implies a bundle. Name one item clearly, explain size choices and included quantity, and use alternate terms naturally in descriptions.
- Never call a pillowcase or cover a pillow in product copy. Amazon wording rules: [[Amazon-Listing-Copy-75-Char-Titles]].
- Plain product titles without decorative quotation marks. The product name is Hug This Pillow Until You Can Hug Me; prose quoting a printed message uses its complete actual words.
- Details gives count, dimensions, actual options, supported material and care. Shipping gives actual policy facts and links. Generic quality or convenience promises add nothing.
- Preserve useful existing copy when the owner says it is good. Naps P-234 body above Details was explicitly retained; its two-cover set differs from single-cover P-284.
- Review an entire article, not only its intro or shopping link. Keep real stories and authors; never invent experiences, testimonials, or product claims.
- Include live page links whenever reporting a correction.

## Image rules
- Use visually matching original listing photos. Check native dimensions, actual design and color, and the rendered image source.
- Bigger unrelated graphics, different colors, packaging, or artificial upscaling cannot fix a blurry product photo.
