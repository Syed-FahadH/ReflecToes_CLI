# Ben's preferences and voice

## Talking to Ben
- Fast and concise. No preamble or filler.
- Prefer useful actions and concrete recommendations over theory.
- Explain technical topics plainly; do not assume Ben is a developer.
- Verify uncertain facts and never invent an answer. Ask only when missing information would materially change the result.
- No em dashes in any written copy.
- Must not sound AI-generated. No emojis, canned hype, or words such as "elevate" and "unlock." Use plain, spoken-word wording.

## ReflecToes copy
- Catchphrase: "Be Safe. Be Seen." (never "Be Seen. Stay Safe.")
- Say reflective strips, gear, or bands. Not "panels".
- Never "trigger biomotion". The gear highlights the biomotion people already have.
- Skip technical jargon unless Ben introduced it first.
- Visual system: near-black #0A0D10, hi-vis yellow-green #D6FF3D, Barlow Condensed Bold. Reflective surfaces are clean flat bright white, no starbursts or lens flares.
- For product imagery, preserve exact product geometry, construction, materials, and logo orientation. Do not invent or mirror features.
- Callout lines and arrows must terminate precisely on the feature they describe.
- Amazon main images contain no text. Secondary listing images may use clear infographic treatment.
- Archived prompts are references, not current specifications. Current Area and SOP rules win when they conflict.
